# QBO 10 Extras (P3) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The cheap P3 items on top of workstreams 01–09: recurring JE templates, bulk A/R write-off, a cash-flow
tile with a simple projection, three customer statement types, a privacy toggle, and firm close-checklist templates.

**Architecture:** Each item is a pure module in `services/accounting/compute/` (tested without a DB) plus a thin
router and a UI panel. Recurring entries and write-offs post through the existing journal-entry path
(`accounting_books.entry_problem` / `new_entry_row` + `audit.record`); write-offs are linked to the open items through
a new `aa_journal_entries.writeoff_items` column the subledger reads as payments. Checklists create ordinary
`aa_tasks`. Privacy is frontend-only.

**Tech Stack:** FastAPI + SQLAlchemy async (Postgres, FORCE RLS), reportlab PDF writer in `subledger_report.py`,
Next.js / React / TS.

**Spec:** `2026-10-16-quickbooks-parity-plan.md` §3 "P3 — Nice to have"; program rules
`2026-10-16-qbo-parity-program.md` row 10.

## Global Constraints

- New tables `aa_recurring_entries`, `aa_checklist_templates`: `enterprise_id` + FORCE RLS firm policy (`rls._FIRM_TABLES`).
- New columns `aa_journal_entries.recurring_id`, `aa_journal_entries.writeoff_items` in the model **and** `schema.py`.
- Every query filters `enterprise_id`; every mutation writes an audit event; services raise `ValueError`, routers `HTTPException`.
- Books-loading routes validate their date window (2000-01-01 … end of next fiscal year, as the reports do).
- Frontend: `seg()` for path segments, `safeId` for ids from URLs, theme CSS vars, a Hint on each new section.

## Review Focus

- A recurring template on the 31st posts on Feb 28 / Apr 30 and goes back to the 31st (anchor day kept).
- Pressing "Post due entries" twice (or two tabs) never posts the same occurrence twice (row lock + `recurring_id`/date check).
- Writing off an invoice that was paid meanwhile, or more than its open balance, is refused; deleting the write-off JE reopens it.
- A write-off dated in a closed month, to a locked / inactive / non-expense account is refused with a clear 400.
- Balance-forward statement: opening = charges − credits before the start; closing = opening + window activity = open-item total at the end.

## Decisions

1. **Recurring entries never post on their own.** There is no scheduler in the app (only extraction jobs), so due
   entries are listed (Books panel, "N due") and post only when a person presses "Post due entries"; each posted JE is
   audited with the template name as the reason. No notification kind added (the "due" chip is where people look).
2. **Catch-up dates in a closed month** post like any typed JE (the books engine turns them into correcting entries on
   the first open day, as JE import does); account / books-start problems refuse the whole post.
3. **Write-off = Dr chosen expense / Cr A/R at the open balance** (net of customer CWT, as the aging shows). Output VAT
   is not reversed (a CPA question; reverse it with a typed JE if wanted). A/R only — bills are not "written off".
   Managers and owners only; the date must be in an open month (refused, not corrected), today or earlier.
4. **Write-off JEs can't be edited** (the item links would drift from the lines); delete and write off again. Deleting
   reopens the items.
5. **Cash projection** = books cash (cash on hand + banks + e-wallets) at the as-of date + A/R − A/P by due date,
   weekly. Overdue items are shown apart and not assumed collected / paid. No books → flows only.
6. **Statement types:** open item (as before); balance forward (opening line, window invoices / payments / write-offs,
   running balance); transaction (window lines only, no opening). Amounts are net of the customer's CWT, matching the
   aging. Aging footer on all three.
7. **Privacy toggle** is per viewer in localStorage, blurs amounts on the Overview tiles and the work board's health
   checks; reports and books are left as they are (they are working screens, not screen-share dashboards).
8. **Checklists** create ordinary tasks; re-applying to the same company + period skips steps whose task title exists.
   Two built-in templates (monthly close, quarter close) are always offered; firm templates are managed on Team by
   owners / managers.
9. **Skipped:** QBO / Xero export (the COA and contacts are trivial but QBO's import columns can't be verified here, and
   the JE export is already covered by the transaction-list / GL XLSX); report email scheduling (no mail infra);
   prepaid schedules (not in this row's list).
10. **New standard account `6140 Bad debts`** (system key `bad_debts`), seeded into existing charts like `6130 Bank
    charges`; it is the write-off panel's default and, unlike engine accounts, can be locked. Any active, unlocked
    expense account may be chosen instead.
11. **"At least 0 days past due" means every open invoice**, due or not (so a whole small-balance clean-up can be picked).
12. **Built during implementation, not in the first draft:** journal entries say `is_writeoff` / `recurring` (chips in
    Books; a write-off shows no Edit button), payments on the contact page show "Written off".
13. **Known gaps (left):** the bank matcher can still offer a written-off invoice as a candidate (its open amount comes
    from the books' payments, which don't include write-offs); recurring entries raise no notification; no tour slides
    were added (each new section has a Hint).
