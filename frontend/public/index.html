    The books, kept as you go.

    The same documents post to each client's journals and general ledger, printed as BIR loose-leaf books. Nobody re-encodes a thing at year end.

    • Six books for VAT-registered clients, four for non-VAT. Page numbers run on across the whole year.
    • Printing a month closes it. Its pages never change; a later fix posts as a correcting entry in the next open month.
    • Official receipts are matched to the invoice they pay, so a sale is never counted twice.
    • Year end is ready to bind: trial balance, financial statements, a cover and the sworn declaration.
