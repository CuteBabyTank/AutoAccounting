Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

AutoAccounting is an online back office for Philippine accounting firms. Firms upload their clients' receipts, invoices, BIR Form 2307s, payroll files and bank statements; AutoAccounting reads them, files them per client and period, prepares BIR returns and keeps the books of accounts. This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

Who is responsible for your information

For the accounts of the people who sign in (firm owners, managers and staff), AutoAccounting decides how that information is used and is the personal information controller.

For the documents and records a firm uploads about its clients (and about those clients' customers, suppliers and employees), the accounting firm is the personal information controller. AutoAccounting processes that information only on the firm's instructions, to provide the service. If you are a firm's client, please send privacy requests about your records to your accounting firm; we will help them respond.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in details. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password and we do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or any other Google data.
  • Firm and team details. Your firm, your role (owner, manager or staff), the clients you are assigned to, and settings you choose.
  • Documents and the data read from them. Files your firm uploads and the details read from them, such as names, Taxpayer Identification Numbers, addresses, amounts, dates, payroll figures and bank transactions. These can include personal information of your clients and of the people they deal with.
  • Records produced in the service. Books of accounts, journal entries, BIR returns, tasks, notes, and an audit log of who changed what and when.
  • Usage and security information. IP address, browser type, the pages and features used, error logs, and the number of pages processed (used for billing where a page rate applies).

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. The app keeps your sign-in session in your browser's session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep each firm's data separate from every other firm's.
  • To read uploaded documents, sort them, prepare returns and books, and show them to the people in your firm who are allowed to see them.
  • To answer questions asked through the in-app assistant, using your firm's own records.
  • To bill for usage, keep the service secure, investigate errors and abuse, and keep an audit trail.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including record-keeping rules for tax records.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

AutoAccounting's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use your Google profile information only to sign you in and to show who did what inside your firm.

How long we keep it

  • Documents, books and returns are kept while your firm uses AutoAccounting and for at least five years, because Philippine tax records must be retained for the period the Bureau of Internal Revenue requires.
  • Database backups are kept for 30 days, with one monthly backup kept for up to six years.
  • Deleted or replaced files can be recovered for up to 30 days before they are removed.
  • When a firm stops using the service it can ask for an export, and for deletion once legal retention periods have ended.

How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and our providers encrypt stored data.
  • Each firm's records are separated in the database by row-level security, and access inside a firm follows the roles the firm assigns.
  • Sign-in is through Google only; we never store passwords.
  • Changes to firm records are written to an audit log that cannot be edited.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs that requires notification, we will inform the affected firm and, where required, the National Privacy Commission, within the periods the law sets.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access, to object, to correct, to have information erased or blocked, to data portability, and to claim damages. To exercise a right, email us at the address below. If the request concerns records your accounting firm uploaded, we will pass it to the firm and help it respond. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Children

AutoAccounting is a business tool for accounting professionals and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. If a change materially affects how we use personal information, we will tell firm owners in the app or by email before it takes effect.
